## Self-care prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: supplied self-care gift-box photograph. Change ONLY the beige fabric/background surface outside and beneath the open black gift box to clean neutral white #FFFFFF. Keep a subtle natural fabric texture and realistic soft shadows, but remove the beige colour cast from that exterior background. Preserve the entire black box, upright black lid, cream crinkle paper inside the box, SANZI face mask sachet and all its lettering, brown satin ruffle, white MARIA hand cream and all labels exactly as photographed. Preserve their positions, colours, shapes, perspective, lighting, portrait composition and framing. Do not recolour the products, packaging or interior paper. No new objects, text, cropping or redesign. Output the same photo with only the surrounding background made white.

## Tea & spices prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: supplied tea and spices gift-box photograph. Change ONLY the beige fabric/background surface outside and underneath the open black gift box to clean neutral white #FFFFFF. Retain subtle natural fabric texture and soft realistic shadows but no beige tint in the exterior surface. Preserve the black box and upright lid, cream crinkle paper inside it, patterned black-and-gold DIRÉ ADDIS tea pack and every label/letter, both Mill & Mortar black spice tins and their coloured labels, all object positions, shapes and colours exactly as in the source photograph. Preserve the original camera angle, lighting, portrait framing and composition. Do not whiten the inside filler, products or black box. No added objects, cropping, redesign or text changes. Only the exterior background becomes white.
